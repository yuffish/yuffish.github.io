I am a third year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, working with [Prof. Mingyu Ding](https://dingmyu.github.io/) and [Prof. Daniel Szafir](https://www.danszafir.com/). Before that, I obtained my master's degree at ShanghaiTech University, advised by [Prof. Dinggang Shen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=v6VYQC8AAAAJ) and [Prof. Zhiming Cui](https://shanghaitech-impact.github.io/). During my Ph.D., I interned at Salesforce AI Research with [Dr. Michael S. Ryoo](http://michaelryoo.com/) and [Dr. Juan Carlos Niebles](https://www.niebles.net/), and at Meta Monetization GenAI with [Dr. Shoufa Chen](https://www.shoufachen.com/).

I am broadly interested in embodied AI and robot learning. My long-term goal is to bridge the gap between simulation and the real world, and to develop agents with human-level perception, reasoning, and behavior. My current research focuses on: 1) VLA and world models for robotic manipulation, 2) synthetic data generation for sim-to-real applications, and 3) 3D/4D perception.
