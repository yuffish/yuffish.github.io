I am a second year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, working with [Prof. Mingyu Ding](https://dingmyu.github.io/). Before that, I obtained my master's degree at ShanghaiTech University, advised by [Prof. Dinggang Shen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=v6VYQC8AAAAJ) and [Prof. Zhiming Cui](https://shanghaitech-impact.github.io/).

I am broadly interested in 3D computer vision and embodied AI. My long-term goal is to bridge the gap between simulation and the real world, and to develop agents with human-level perception and reasoning. My current focus includes: 1) synthetic data generation for sim-to-real applications, and 2) 3D/4D perception on robots and AI glasses from egocentric viewpoints.
