I am a second year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Before that, I obtained my master's degree at ShanghaiTech University, advised by [Dinggang Shen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=v6VYQC8AAAAJ) and [Zhiming Cui](https://shanghaitech-impact.github.io/).

My research interests lie in 3D computer vision and embodied AI. I focus on bridging the gap between simulation and the real world, as well as between agents and humans. I am broadly interested in developing spatial intelligence, embodied intelligence, and generative AI.
